📲 Add to Home Screen

Play fullscreen, no browser bars

Zoomies GP runs as an app — add it to your Home Screen to play.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  3. Open Zoomies GP from your Home Screen

How to Play

  • Tilt phone to steer
  • Left slider: up = gas, down = brake / reverse
  • ⬆ Hold Jump while turning to drift — longer drift, bigger boost
  • 🐾 Shoot hairballs to spin out rivals
  • 🛡️ Hold Shield to block hairballs
  • 💨 Boost — a turbo cat toot (recharges)
  • First to finish all laps wins!

Desktop: arrows · Space jump · F shoot · B boost · Shift shield
