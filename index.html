    🖥 Display

    Graphics

    Low — older phones (simplest effects, bare verges). Balanced — most phones: the full living world (grass, motes) without the priciest effects. Medium — newer phones (full effects, 60fps, stays cool). High — big GPUs: real-time shadows, longer draw distance and a denser, livelier world, still 60fps. (Extra density lands on the next launch.)

    Frame rate

    Auto follows the display (60 on 100Hz+ screens). A lower cap runs cooler and longer on battery — the game picks the nearest even divisor of your screen's refresh so pacing stays smooth.

    Battery saver

    Caps the frame rate (45 on Steam Deck, 30 elsewhere, unless you set one above), renders menus at 20fps, lowers the resolution ceiling, freezes the sun shadow map, skips the sun rays and ambient motes and pauses when the window loses focus. On by default on Steam Deck.

    Versus effects (2P only)

    Full glow, sun rays & colour grade in 2-player split screen (3–4 players always run lean). Heavier: both halves run the whole effect stack — watch the FPS counter and see if your machine holds up.

    Compatibility mode

    Use if the game crashes — switches to more stable graphics (restarts).

    🎮 Controls

    Controller rumble

    Controller — stick/d-pad steer (and browse the menus) · RT gas · LT brake · A hop & drift · X shoot (hold to charge) · B boost · Y milk · LB/RB shield · Start pause.

    Keyboard — A/D or ◀▶ steer · W/S or ▲▼ gas/brake · Space hop & drift · F shoot · B boost · M milk · Shift shield · P or Esc pause.

    Touch — tilt to steer · left slider gas/brake · right buttons for tricks.

    Versus (2–4P, desktop) — controllers are dealt in player order (P1 gets the first pad), and the first player without a pad gets the keyboard. The start line shows who has what.
