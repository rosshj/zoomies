How to Play

📱Tilt your phone to steer
🕹️Left slider — up gas, down brake
⬆Hold Jump in a turn to drift — longer drift, bigger boost
🐾Shoot hairballs to spin out rivals
🛡️Hold Shield to block hairballs
💨Boost — a turbo cat toot (recharges)
🏁First to finish all laps wins!
🕹️Left stick or d-pad to steer
🎮RT gas · LT brake / reverse
⬆Hold Ⓐ in a turn to drift — longer drift, bigger boost
🐾Ⓧ shoots hairballs (hold to charge)
🛡️Hold LB / RB to shield
💨Ⓑ boost (recharges) · Ⓨ spill milk
🏁First to finish all laps wins!

    Settings

    Music
    Sound FX
    Graphics

    Low saves power. Balanced is recommended for most phones. Medium adds richer effects. High adds the most detail and uses more power. Some detail changes apply on the next launch.

    Frame rate

    Auto matches your display. Lower limits save battery. The game uses a compatible frame rate for smooth motion.

    Battery saver

    Runs cooler and uses less battery by reducing frame rate and visual effects. Enabled by default on Steam Deck.

    Versus effects (2P only)

    Richer effects in two-player races. Turn off for smoother play on slower devices. Three- and four-player races always use simpler effects.

    Compatibility mode

    Use if the game crashes — switches to more stable graphics (restarts).

    Steering indicator
    Center tilt steering

    Hold the phone in your driving grip, then tap.

    Controller rumble

    Controller — stick/d-pad steer (and browse the menus) · RT gas · LT brake · A hop & drift · X shoot (hold to charge) · B boost · Y milk · LB/RB shield · Start pause.

    Keyboard — A/D or ◀▶ steer · W/S or ▲▼ gas/brake · Space hop & drift · F shoot · B boost · M milk · Shift shield · P or Esc pause.

    Touch — tilt to steer · left slider gas/brake · right buttons for tricks.

    Versus (2–4P, desktop) — controllers are dealt in player order (P1 gets the first pad), and the first player without a pad gets the keyboard. The start line shows who has what.

    Back up or restore progress

    Your treats, unlocks and trophies as a copy-paste code — copy it here, paste it on another device (or the web version) and press Restore.
